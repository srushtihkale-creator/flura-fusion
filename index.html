Plant Care Guide

Simple, trustworthy guidance for joyful plant parenting.

💧 The Golden Rule of Watering

More indoor plants suffer from over-watering than under-watering. Always insert your index finger 2 inches into the soil. If damp, wait! When watering, pour slowly until water drains freely from the base drainage hole.

☀️ Deciphering Sunlight

Bright Indirect Light: Within 3-5 feet of an unshaded window.
Low Light: Interior hallways or north-facing rooms (Snake Plant and ZZ Plant thrive here).
Direct Sun: Essential for flowering lavender, jade plants, and balcony vegetable seeds.

🏺 Potting & Drainage

Always ensure your pot has a drainage hole. If using decorative metal or marble pots without holes, place the plant in a nursery plastic pot inside the decorative container (cachepot method).
